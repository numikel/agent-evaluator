# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, keep feedback about the work and not the person, and
make room for people who are new to the project or to the topic.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, publishing others' private
information and sustained disruption of discussions.

Reports go privately to the maintainer through GitHub (`@numikel`). Reports are handled
confidentially and the maintainer may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban anyone who
violates these rules.
